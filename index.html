Technology

A platform built to move at market speed.

  • Speed to scale. Fanatics' infrastructure, tuned for the UAE's 5G-first audience and a seamless move from live roulette to the sportsbook.
  • One experience, every screen. A responsive web platform with a persistent play bar across Casino, Sportsbook and Racing.
  • Compliance in the core. Age, identity and location verification engineered into the stack, not bolted on.
Responsible gaming

Protection designed in from the first screen.

  • Player controls throughout. Deposit, time and reality limits available before and during every session.
  • Wellbeing partners. Integrated support, including Takalam, for players whose play becomes unsustainable.
  • Eligibility enforced. 21+, physically in the UAE, outside sensitive areas, with no identity or location masking.
